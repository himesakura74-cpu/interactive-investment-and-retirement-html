Retirement Savings Formulas Used

Projected Retirement Balance (Future Value)FV = P × (1 + i)n + PMT × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i]
Total ContributionsContributions = Starting Savings + (Monthly Contribution × Number of Months)
Investment EarningsEarnings = Projected Retirement Balance − Total Contributions
Share from Investment GrowthGrowth Share (%) = Investment Earnings ÷ Projected Retirement Balance × 100

P = starting savings; PMT = monthly contribution; i = annual return ÷ 12 (as a decimal); n = years × 12. Contributions are made at month-end.
