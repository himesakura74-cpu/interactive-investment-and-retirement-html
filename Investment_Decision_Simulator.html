The chart begins at the initial cash outflow (Year 0). A value above zero at the end indicates a positive NPV.

Assumes constant annual cash inflows received at each year-end, no salvage value, and a constant discount rate. Simple payback does not discount cash flows; payback longer than the project duration means the cost is not recovered during the project.

Investment Formulas Used

Net Present Value (NPV)NPV = −Initial Investment + Σ [Annual Cash Flow ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)t]
Present Value of Cash InflowsPV = Σ [Annual Cash Flow ÷ (1 + Discount Rate)t]
Profitability IndexPI = Present Value of Cash Inflows ÷ Initial Investment
Simple Payback PeriodPayback (years) = Initial Investment ÷ Annual Cash Flow
Investment Decision RuleAccept if NPV ≥ $0; reject if NPV < $0

Σ means add the discounted cash flows for each year; t = year number (1 through project duration). Payback is undiscounted.
